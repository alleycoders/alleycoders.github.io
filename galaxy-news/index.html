Product Philosophy

Why Choose Galaxy News?

Designed ground-up for readers who demand total control over their news feeds without algorithmic clutter or privacy compromises.

feed

Zero Pre-loaded Feeds

No promoted media outlets or sponsored content. Import OPML or add your exact custom RSS/Atom feeds from day one.

bolt

Flutter Performance

Engineered in Flutter for fluid 60fps/120fps scrolling, minimal resource usage, and instantaneous local feed parsing.

visibility_off

Zero Telemetry

No user tracking, third-party analytics, or remote data collection. All feed states remain stored strictly on your device.

Material You Interface

Seamlessly adapts to modern Material Design 3 guidelines with light/dark theme modes and clean, readable article views.
